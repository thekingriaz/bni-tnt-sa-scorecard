1. Purpose and roles

The SA Scorecard app replaces the monthly Google Sheet. Admins enter each month's data, the app computes scores, bands, ranks and payouts, and SAs see only their own results after the month is published. This guide is the complete monthly procedure.

RoleCan do
AdminOpen months, upload BNI Connect exports, enter manual inputs, sync Jotform, publish/unpublish, manage users and settings, see all 14 scores.
SASee own scorecard, rank and region average for published months only.
SDCSame, on the Support DC tab. Scored on the SDC KPIs (section 9).

The admin console has an SA | SDC switch at the top right. Everything on the Months page belongs to the programme selected there; each month is opened and published separately per programme. Sections 3 to 8 describe the SA month; section 9 describes the SDC month.

Golden rules. (1) Never publish before every chapter has a Member Score, Retention %, LTRT and Jotform status. (2) Upload Chapter Statistics before the Training Report (training % needs the member count). (3) Published months are frozen; unpublish, fix, publish again.

2. Logging in

  1. Open https://sa.bnitnt.in, click the Admin tab, sign in with your email and password.
  2. Change your password on first login: Users → your name → Set password.
  3. Three menus: Months (monthly work), Settings (commission, thresholds, online event list), Users (SAs, PINs, admins).

3. Monthly procedure (first week of each month, for the previous month)

Step 1: Export four files from BNI Connect

ReportBNI Connect path and parametersFiles
Chapter StatisticsReports → Region Meeting Statistics → Chapter Statistics. Region: Tirunelveli, then Nagercoil & Tuticorin. From = To = the month (e.g. 10/2026). Export to Excel.2 (one per region)
Member Training ReportReports → Region Member Training Report. Region: both, one at a time. All events selected. From = 1st of the month, To = last day of the month. Show: Members who have attended. Export to Excel.2 (one per region)

The files download as .xls. Upload them exactly as downloaded; do not open and re-save in Excel. A file covering more than one month is rejected.

Step 2: Upload

  1. Months → drop all four files on the upload box (or click it and select). The app reads the month and the region from each file and opens that month automatically.
  2. Order matters: Chapter Statistics first, then Training. If you drop all four together, the app handles them in sequence; if a training file shows "no member count yet", re-upload that training file after the Chapter Statistics.
  3. Check the upload log: "8 chapters updated" (Tirunelveli) and "6 chapters updated" (Nagercoil & Tuticorin). A chapter listed as "not found" means its name in BNI Connect changed; tell the ED.

What the uploads fill in: Members (Mem Avg), 1-2-1 per member, Online % and Offline % (unique members trained ÷ members; the split follows the online event list in Settings).

Step 3: Sync Jotform

  1. Click Sync Jotform. The app pulls that month's "BNI Chapter Visit Report" submissions and ticks every chapter where the submitter's name matches the SA's Jotform name (Users page).
  2. Read the result list. "Submitted by X, no match for Y" means the SA wrote their name differently: either fix the Jotform name in Users and sync again, or set the Jotform cell to Done by hand. Manual changes are kept on later syncs.

Step 4: Enter the manual inputs (per chapter)

ColumnSourceRule
Member scoreMonthly Member Traffic Light reportThe SA's own score as a member (0 to 100).
Retention %3+1 Traffic Lights workbook (.xlsx), uploadedDrop the file on the upload box. The app takes the First Year Retention column (the SDC month gets Overall Retention from the same file). Chapters under 12 months show n/a. Type a value only to correct the report.
LTRTLTRT / DCRT attendance sheet (also in BNI Connect)Attended / Not attended. If no LTRT or DCRT was scheduled that month, mark Attended so nobody is penalised.

Cells save when you leave them. Score, band, payout and rank update immediately. You may also override Online %, Offline % and 1-2-1 if an upload was wrong; the override is kept until the next upload of that report.

Step 5: Review and publish

  1. Every row must have a Member score, Retention % (or n/a), LTRT and Jotform value. A blank counts as zero and will be questioned.
  2. Check the region average and the band spread look sane against last month.
  3. Click Publish month. SAs see it instantly. Payout figures are now frozen for that month.
  4. Found a mistake after publishing? Unpublish → correct → Publish again. Tell the affected SA.
Time budget: about 45 minutes a month once the four files are exported: 5 min uploads, 2 min Jotform, 25 min manual inputs, 10 min review.

Payout

Your monthly payout has two parts: how many members paid in your chapter that month, and which band you landed in.

StepCalculation
1. Members paidNew member inductions + renewals paid in that month (a renewal due 1 Nov but paid on 15 Oct counts in October)
2. Base commissionMembers paid × 2.5% of ₹49,040 = Members paid × ₹1,226
3. Band multiplierBase commission × your band multiplier (1.00 / 0.80 / 0.60 / 0.35 / 0.15)

Example: 1 new member + 3 renewals paid in October = 4 members × ₹1,226 = ₹4,904 base. Score 75 (Green, 0.80×) pays ₹3,923. The same month at Gold would pay ₹4,904. At Red it pays ₹1,716. The band costs you real money.

The fee and percentage are set by the regional office and may be revised. The dashboard always shows the current figures.

9. The SDC month (Support Director Consultants)

Switch the console to SDC. The SDC month is a separate page with its own publish button, but it shares the chapter list, the Chapter Statistics upload and the New / Renewals counts with the SA month.

Step 1: Open the month

Months → pick the month → Open month. The app creates one row per chapter and the weekly grid: one cell per chapter meeting date (Ainthinai Tue, Bamboo Kottai Wed, Cmy Veli Thu, and so on; meeting days are in Settings).

Step 2: Every Friday evening during the month

  1. Click Sync Jotform. Tele-bridge submissions are matched to the meeting week by chapter and meeting date. TB ✓ only if submitted by 09:30 AM IST two days after the meeting; TB ✗ once that deadline passes with nothing on time; blank while the window is still open. Expand Show all Tele-bridge submissions to see every submission, its deadline and why it did or didn't count.
  2. PALMS: drop the Friday Last Meeting Entered screenshot on the PALMS on time · Friday check box. The app reads it (a few seconds), fills one line per chapter and sets the report date. Check the 14 lines against the screenshot (fix any wrong date, type any chapter it lists as "Not read"), then click Apply PALMS. Each chapter's meeting that week is ticked ✓ if it was entered, ✗ if the last meeting entered is older. Slightly misread names are matched to the right chapter automatically. Single cells can still be clicked by hand.
  3. If a chapter did not meet this week (holiday), click no meeting under that cell. The week drops out of both denominators.
  4. A wrong automatic tick can be clicked to change it; manual ticks survive later syncs.

10. Data rules worth remembering

  • Training counts unique members. A member attending three workshops counts once.
  • 1-2-1 is per member per month, straight from BNI Connect. The old sheet's "2 per week" entries were never real; do not type a number by hand unless the upload failed.
  • Payout counts members by payment month, not by renewal date.
  • History belongs to the person, not the chapter. A new SA starts with a blank history.
  • The Google Sheet is now an archive. The app is the record.
  • Go-live is October 2026 for both programmes. Earlier months are not scored or shown. September 2026 is marked baseline and cannot be published.
  • SDC net growth is the month's joined minus left from Membership Activity by Chapter, not the trailing "Growth" figure printed on the Traffic Lights report.
  • Tele-bridge weeks are matched by the meeting date typed on the form, not by the date the form was submitted.
