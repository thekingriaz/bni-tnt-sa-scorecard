1. What this is

The SDC Scorecard is your personal performance page. Every month the regional office publishes your score across eight KPIs, your band, your rank among the 14 Support DCs, the region average, and the commission you earned. It replaces the SDC Google Sheet. Every point is explained on the page, and every point tells you what to do next month.

Why it exists. A Support DC owns the health of a chapter: is it growing, is it GREEN, are members renewing, is the Leadership Team being coached every week. The scorecard rewards exactly those things. Nothing on it is outside your influence.

2. Logging in

  1. Open https://sa.bnitnt.in on your phone or laptop. Save it to your home screen.
  2. Tap the Support DC tab, pick your chapter and enter your PIN.
  3. Forgot your PIN? Message the BNI TNT office. They reset it in seconds. Never share your PIN.

If you support two chapters, you have two logins, one per chapter. Each chapter has its own scorecard and its own rank.

5. Your payout

Your monthly payout has two parts: how many members paid in your chapter that month, and which band you landed in.

StepCalculation
1. Members paidNew member inductions + renewals paid in that month in your chapter (the same count the office enters for the SA programme)
2. Base commissionMembers paid × 7.5% of ₹49,040 = Members paid × ₹3,678
3. Band multiplierBase commission × your band multiplier (1.00 / 0.80 / 0.60 / 0.35 / 0.15)

Example: 1 new member + 3 renewals paid in October = 4 members × ₹3,678 = ₹14,712 base. Score 75 (Green, 0.80×) pays ₹11,770. The same month at Gold pays ₹14,712. At Red it pays ₹5,149.

The fee and percentage are set by the regional office and may be revised. The dashboard always shows the current figures.

6. How to score high: the playbook

Work the KPIs in this order. The first four are entirely in your hands and together are worth 35 points, the difference between Red and Gold.

6.1 Tele-Bridge call every week: 10 points

  • After every chapter meeting, hold the tele-bridge call with the Leadership Team and submit the Support DC Tele-bridge Call form within 48 hours: by 09:30 AM IST two days after the meeting (meeting Tuesday 20 Oct, deadline Thursday 22 Oct 09:30). A submission after that scores 0 for the week. Pick the right chapter and the right meeting date on the form: the app matches your submission to that meeting week.
  • Two submissions for the same week count once. A missed week cannot be made up later.
  • If the chapter did not meet (holiday), tell the office so the week is marked "no meeting" and does not count against you.

6.2 PALMS on time every week: 10 points

  • Chase the Secretary/Treasurer until PALMS is in on time. The office ticks each week on Friday evening. Late is the same as missing.

6.3 Chapter Visit Report within 24 hours: 10 points

  • Visit the chapter at least once in the month and submit the BNI Chapter Visit Report on the day of the visit or the next day. Later than that scores 0, even if the visit happened.
  • Write your name on the form exactly as the office has it on record ("Visited By"). A different spelling does not match.

6.4 LTRT / DCRT attendance: 5 points

  • Be in the room. If you cannot attend, inform the office before the meeting.

6.5 Your own Member Score: 10 points

  • Your own Traffic Light score must be 80 or more. You cannot ask a chapter to go GREEN while you are AMBER.

6.6 Chapter GREEN on All India Traffic Lights: 15 points

  • GREEN is 70+ on the BNI Traffic Lights. The six components are the same for every chapter: size, growth, retention, referrals per member, visitors, attendance.
  • Read your chapter's Path to Green report every month and work the weakest component with the Leadership Team.

6.7 Chapter Net Growth: 20 points

  • +2 members in a month is the full 20. +1 is 10. Flat or negative is 0, however many visitors came.
  • Growth is inductions minus drops. Work both ends: a visitor pipeline with the Visitor Host team, and a renewal conversation 90 days out with every member due.

6.8 Overall Renewal Rate 80%+: 20 points

  • This is the chapter's overall retention figure on the 3+1 Traffic Lights, a rolling number. It moves slowly, so every renewal saved this month keeps paying for a year.
  • Know every member's renewal date. 90 days before: a 1-2-1 with you. 60 days: check their numbers with them. 30 days: renewal conversation with the Membership Committee.
A realistic Gold month. Tele-Bridge every week (10) + PALMS every week (10) + visit report within 24 h (10) + LTRT (5) + member score 80+ (10) + chapter GREEN (15) + net +2 (20) = 80 = Green. Add renewal rate 70%+ (10) and you are at 90, Gold. The weekly disciplines alone are 35 points.
