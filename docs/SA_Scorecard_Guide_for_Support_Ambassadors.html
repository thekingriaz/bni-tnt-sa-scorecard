1. What this is

The SA Scorecard is your personal performance page. Every month the regional office publishes your score across six KPIs, your band, your rank among the 14 Support Ambassadors, the region average, and the commission you earned. It replaces the old Google Sheet. Nothing is hidden: every point is explained on the page, and every point tells you what to do next month.

Why it exists. An SA is a member first and a chapter's support system second. The scorecard rewards exactly the things that make a chapter healthy: members trained, members retained, 1-2-1s happening, and you showing up. Score high and the chapter grows. There is no trick to it.

2. Logging in

  1. Open https://sa.bnitnt.in on your phone or laptop. Save it to your home screen.
  2. On the Support Ambassador tab, pick your chapter and enter your PIN.
  3. Forgot your PIN? Message the BNI TNT office. They reset it in seconds. Never share your PIN.

If you support two chapters, you have two logins, one per chapter. Each chapter has its own scorecard and its own rank.

5. Your payout

Your monthly payout has two parts: how many members paid in your chapter that month, and which band you landed in.

StepCalculation
1. Members paidNew member inductions + renewals paid in that month (a renewal due 1 Nov but paid on 15 Oct counts in October)
2. Base commissionMembers paid × 2.5% of ₹49,040 = Members paid × ₹1,226
3. Band multiplierBase commission × your band multiplier (1.00 / 0.80 / 0.60 / 0.35 / 0.15)

Example: 1 new member + 3 renewals paid in October = 4 members × ₹1,226 = ₹4,904 base. Score 75 (Green, 0.80×) pays ₹3,923. The same month at Gold would pay ₹4,904. At Red it pays ₹1,716. The band costs you real money.

The fee and percentage are set by the regional office and may be revised. The dashboard always shows the current figures.

6. How to score high: the playbook

Work the KPIs in this order. The first three are entirely in your hands and together are worth 25 raw points, the difference between Red and Gold.

6.1 Chapter Visit Report on Jotform: 10 points, 10 minutes

  • Submit the BNI Chapter Visit Report form once every month, before the last day of the month. The system picks it up automatically.
  • Write your name on the form exactly as the office has it on record ("Visited By" / "Name"). A different spelling does not match and you lose the 10 points.
  • In September 2026, 9 of 14 SAs missed this. It was the single cheapest 10 points in the region.

6.2 LTRT / DCRT attendance: 5 points

  • Be in the room. If you cannot attend, inform the office before the meeting. Not attending is 0, and it is noticed.

6.3 Your own Member Score: 10 points

  • Your own Traffic Light score must be 80 or more. You cannot ask members to do what you don't do.
  • Attendance, referrals given, visitors invited, 1-2-1s, CEUs, TYFCB: the same six things you coach members on.

6.4 1-2-1 Activity: 10 points, needs 8.66 per member per month

  • This is 2 per member per week. In September only one chapter in the region cleared it. It is hard, and it is the most important number in BNI.
  • Run a 1-2-1 challenge with the Membership Committee: weekly leaderboard in the chapter group, recognition at the meeting.
  • Pair every new member with 4 scheduled 1-2-1s in their first month. New members pull the chapter average up fast.
  • Make sure members record their 1-2-1s in BNI Connect. Unrecorded 1-2-1s do not exist for scoring.

6.5 Training Attendance: 20 points, needs 20% of members

  • Counted as unique members who attended any BNI training in the month, divided by chapter size. A 50-member chapter needs 10 different members in training that month.
  • Book MSP for every new member in their induction month. That alone is 2 to 4 members.
  • Push one workshop per month (Feature Presentation, Referral Skills, Power Team) and get the Education Coordinator to announce it twice.
  • Online trainings count the same as offline. They are the easiest way to reach 20%.

6.6 1st-Year Retention: 25 points, the biggest prize

  • Members who leave in year one are members nobody looked after. Your job is to know every first-year member by name and by renewal date.
  • 90 days before renewal: a 1-2-1 with you. 60 days: check their referral and TYFCB numbers with them. 30 days: renewal conversation with the Membership Committee.
  • At 75% you get the full 25 points. At 69% you get nothing. The gap between those two is usually one or two members.
A realistic Gold month. Member score 80+ (10) + 20% trained (20) + retention 75%+ (25) + LTRT (5) + Jotform (10) = 70 raw = 87.5 on the dashboard = Gold, even with the 1-2-1 target missed. Gold is reachable without a perfect month.
